About me

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science (NLP group) at the University of Sheffield, UK. I use Natural Language Processing, Linguistics and some Musicology to work on song lyrics, under the supervision of Rob Gaizauskas and Mark Hepple.

I moved in Sheffield back in 2018, when I started an MA in Cognitive Studies, combining Computer Science with Linguistics and Cognition. Prior to this, I had obtained a BSc in Computer Science from the University of Piraeus in Greece.

When I am not working at the office, I will be either in the Diamond labs working as a GTA or at a park in Sheffield, where I will most probably be studying some language, reading Dostoyevsky, or writing lyrics.

Through working as a GTA I developed a passion for teaching. I have attended a range of workshops offered by the University in order to improve my teaching skills, while I have also worked as a volunteer instructor in Code First Girls (now working part-time as a Nanodegree Assistant Instructor) and as a volunteer Python Guru for Cookies & Code.




Trivia

Since I work with song lyrics, you might have guessed that I love music and... lyrics! I enjoy writing lyrics to express myself every now and then, and I am glad that I worked together with the art-rock band Pulse on the song Drifting Apart (Spotify), the lyrics of which are written by me.

I also like walking and taking pictures of my surroundings. I am especially in love with Sheffield, so my Instagram account is full of photos of this wonderful city. I love the imperfections in my photos: they remind me that there is always room for improvement and they help me see my progress as an amateur photographer through time.

Last but not least, I love languages and learning about different cultures. It is actually fascinating how cultural features can be expressed through language, so this is probably the main reason why I like learning foreign languages. I was born and raised in Piraeus, Greece, so I am a native speaker of Greek. At school I was taught English and French, and I also had a very short course in Italian. I have been learning Russian by myself for some time, as it is one of my favourite languages. Finally, I recently reached level B1 in German. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in language exchange or if you work on a language-related project!
