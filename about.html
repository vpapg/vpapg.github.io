About me

I am a Teaching Associate in the School of Computer Science at the University of Sheffield. My academic interests include Computational Linguistics, Natural Language Processing, Music Information Retrieval, Machine Learning, Computer Science Education and AI Literacy.

Previously, I worked as a Teaching Associate at the University of Nottingham and as a programming instructor with Code First Girls.

Research

I am also a PhD candidate in the Natural Language Processing group. I use Natural Language Processing and Music Information Retrieval to work on song lyrics, under the supervision of Prof. Rob Gaizauskas and Dr Mark Hepple.
