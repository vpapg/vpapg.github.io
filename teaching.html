Teaching

I have worked as a Graduate Teaching Assistant (GTA) for the following modules at the University of Sheffield:

  • COM1005 Machines and Intelligence (Undergraduate, Autumn 2019-2021)
  • COM160/161 Introduction to Programming and Problem Solving (Undergraduate, Autumn 2019-2021)
  • COM2009/3009 Robotics (Undergraduate, Spring 2020-2021)
  • COM3110/4115 Text Processing (Undergraduate, Autumn 2019-2021)
  • COM6115 Text Processing (Postgraduate, Autumn 2019-2020)
  • COM3528 Cognitive and Biomimetic Robotics (Undergraduate, Spring 2020,2022)
My responsibilities included: supporting students in the weekly lab sessions; marking assessments; occasionally, testing the material to be taught for particular sessions with the course leaders and other GTAs and contributing to it (example). The programming language used in all modules is Python.

I have also volunteered as a co-instructor in three cohorts of "Introduction to Python" courses offered by Code First Girls (CFG) and as a Python Guru in monthly sessions taking place at the University of Sheffield by Cookies & Code.

Currently, apart from COM3528, I am also working part-time as an Assistant Instructor for the Software Development Nanodegree offered by CFG.
